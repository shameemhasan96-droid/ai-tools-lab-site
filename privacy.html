Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

AI Tools Lab Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the AI Tools Lab YouTube channel. It is used only by that owner, on the owner's own Google account, YouTube channel and Facebook Page ("AI Tools Lab Bangla").

What the app accesses

  • YouTube upload permission: to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel.
  • YouTube read-only and YouTube Analytics read-only permission: to read the owner's own channel statistics (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources) so that future videos can be improved.
  • Facebook Page permissions (pages_manage_posts, pages_manage_engagement, pages_read_engagement, pages_read_user_content, pages_show_list, read_insights): to publish the owner's own videos, Reels and posts to the owner's own Facebook Page, to post the owner's own comment under them, and to read the Page's own engagement statistics.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only for the purposes above. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored in the owner's private, access-controlled storage (a local file and an encrypted secrets store) and are never published.
  • Analytics data is stored only in the owner's private files.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app does not collect personal data from visitors or followers of the Page, and it does not store comments or messages written by other people.

Your choices

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in Facebook under Settings → Business Integrations. See also data deletion instructions.

Contact

Questions: please contact the channel through its YouTube page.
