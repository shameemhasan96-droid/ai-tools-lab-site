Data Deletion Instructions

AI Tools Lab Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the AI Tools Lab channel and Facebook Page. It does not collect or store personal data about visitors, followers or other Facebook users.

To remove the app's access and data

  1. In Facebook, open Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites), select "AI Tools Lab Poster", and choose Remove. This revokes the app's access to the Page.
  2. The only data the app keeps is the owner's own access token and the owner's own publishing logs. Removing the app, as above, invalidates the token; the logs contain no personal data of other people.

If you believe the app holds data about you, contact the Page through its Facebook Page and we will respond and delete it.
